# Parts

This directory contains all individual SolidWorks part files used throughout the project.

The components are organized according to the engine subsystem they belong to rather than by part type. This structure follows the assembly hierarchy of the engine and makes navigation more intuitive.

## Folder Structure

- **lower block** – Engine block, crankshaft, oil pan, and lower-end components.
- **piston assembly** – Pistons, connecting rods, wrist pins, and related components.
- **cylinder head** – Cylinder heads, camshaft, valves, valve cover, and associated components.
- **rocker arm** – Rocker arm mechanism and related hardware.
- **intake system** – Air filters, intake manifold, turbocharger, and intake piping.
- **exhaust system** – Exhaust manifolds and associated exhaust components.
- **front cover** – Front cover and belt drive components.
